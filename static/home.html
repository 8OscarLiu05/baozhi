02 — The problem

Insurance runs on fragmented answers.

Agents, customers and contact centers ask the same questions about waiting periods, exclusions and surrender values. Each tool answers on its own, and nobody can show where an answer came from.

  • InconsistentSame clause, different wording across channels.
  • UntraceableModel output without a citation cannot be reviewed.
  • UngovernedPromises like “this will definitely pay” slip through.

05 — Routing

Three routes,
chosen per request.

The core decides how much the model is allowed to do before it does anything.

General consultation1 model call

The question alone is sent. Company and policy records stay out.

Professional lookupUp to 1 call

Authorized fields answer first. A matched fact may receive one checked explanation.

Role-based answerEvidence first

Fields and excerpts set the answer. Unsafe or unsupported model text is dropped.

06 — Evidence

Every answer,
traceable to the clause.

Numbers must be found in the current fields or excerpts. If the model writes a figure that cannot be matched, its sentence is dropped and the field template answers instead.

07 — Governance

Guardrails
before eloquence.

Four kinds of statements are never allowed to stand as conclusions. When one is detected, the model is not followed and the call is flagged for review.

  • 01Claim promises“It will definitely pay” becomes review conditions and a human path.
  • 02Guaranteed returnsDividends and illustrations are never stated as certain.
  • 03Deposit comparisonsNo “better than a savings account” framing.
  • 04DisparagementExaggeration is logged and sent to sampling.

Append-only audit: every call is retained, and reviewers add dispositions without rewriting the original answer.
